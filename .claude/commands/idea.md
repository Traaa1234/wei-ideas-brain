Follow the Idea section of AGENTS.md for: $ARGUMENTS

Search the wiki first; if a dossier already exists, update it. Otherwise
create it with `wiki.py new idea "<short title>"` and record the idea in my
exact words. Link every existing source, concept and entity that bears on it,
label everything else [hypothesis] or [guess], and propose the cheapest
falsification test. Use `wiki.py related <page>` to find overlapping
ideas and tell me how this one relates to them. Do not decide for me. Then
`wiki.py index`, `wiki.py graph` and `wiki.py log idea`.
