# Value traps

## What it is

A stock whose low multiple reflects real trouble (falling earnings, debt,
political interference) rather than mispricing. The price stays low or falls
further.

## What the sources say

- Weak Brazilian earnings growth in recent years is one reason the market's
  discount has persisted ([Siblis](https://siblisresearch.com/data/ibovespa-pe-ratio/)) [source, from search summary; verify].

## Warning signs

- Earnings estimates being cut while the price is flat. [hypothesis]
- Dividend paid out of debt or above earnings. [hypothesis]
- State control that can redirect profits ([[brazil-country-risk]]). [hypothesis]
