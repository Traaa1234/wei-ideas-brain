# China country risk

## What it is

The extra return investors demand for owning Chinese companies through US
listings. None of the general points below is sourced in this wiki yet.

- Many Chinese ADRs own their Chinese business through contracts (a "VIE"
  structure), not shares. [hypothesis; to source]
- US delisting rules and US-China tension can force sales or block listings. [hypothesis; to source]
- Chinese regulators can change the rules for whole sectors quickly
  (education, internet, finance). [hypothesis; to source]
