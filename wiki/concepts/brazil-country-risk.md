# Brazil country risk

## What it is

The extra return investors demand for holding Brazilian assets: fiscal
deficits, politics, high interest rates and a volatile real (BRL).

## What the sources say

- Itaú BBA's 2026 bull case relies on the Selic falling from 15% toward
  12.75% by December 2026 ([Investidor10](https://investidor10.com.br/noticias/itau-bba-projeta-ibovespa-em-185-mil-pontos-em-2026-117963/)) [source, from search summary; verify].
- Fiscal risk, high rates and election-year volatility are the main bear
  arguments (same broker coverage) [source, from search summary; verify].
