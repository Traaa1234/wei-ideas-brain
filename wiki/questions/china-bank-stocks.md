# Which Chinese bank stocks can Wei buy and how cheap are they

Asked 2026-10-27: "help me filter china's bank stocks". Research notes, not advice.

## Short answer

- **No Chinese bank is listed on NYSE, Nasdaq or AMEX.** Finviz's financial
  sector for China and Hong Kong (27 and 22 names, 2026-10-27) holds consumer
  lenders, brokers, insurers and blank-check shells, with no "Banks" industry at all
  ([Finviz](https://finviz.com/screener.ashx?v=111&f=geo_china,sec_financial&o=pe)).
- The big state banks trade in the US **over the counter (OTC)** as ADRs, in USD.
  OTC is not an exchange listing, so this breaks Wei's "US-listed" rule unless
  Wei widens it. [Wei's call]
- On the figures found they are cheap: about 6-8x earnings, 0.5-0.8x book
  value, 4-5% dividend yields (table below).

## How to filter banks (different from other stocks)

The rules in [[value-screening]] do not fit banks: debt is their raw material,
so Debt/Equity says little. Draft bank rules [Claude's draft; Wei to confirm]:

1. P/B below 1 and P/E below 8.
2. ROE at least 10% (a bank earning 10% on equity at 0.6x book is cheap only if the ROE lasts).
3. Dividend yield at least 4% with a payout ratio below 40%. [payout data not yet found]
4. Bad-loan (NPL) ratio stable or falling and capital (CET1) ratio comfortably above the regulator's minimum. [data not yet found]
5. Net interest margin not falling fast (CCB 1.36%, above).
6. Enough OTC trading volume to buy and sell at a fair price. [check bid-ask spread at the broker]
